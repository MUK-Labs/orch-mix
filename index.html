Listen your way into the orchestra.

  1. Build your library. Remember each folder containing synchronized stems and one .orchmix.json. Choose it from Your pieces next time. Chrome or Edge may ask you to renew folder access.
  2. Explore. Click an orchestra area to turn its sound on or off. The fader level stays unchanged. All off silences the orchestra; press again to restore the selection. Random instrument adds one muted part. S solos a part. Coloured light follows audible energy.
  3. Guess the piece. Random excerpt chooses an accessible saved folder and a passage, starts playing with every part muted, and hides the title. Turn on parts one at a time. Reveal piece shows the answer without changing your mix.
  4. Use your controller. Open MIDI controller, connect, click Learn below a mixer channel and move a hardware fader or knob. Use up to 16 volume controls at once; switch banks for more parts. Learn separate CC buttons for Play / pause, All off / restore and Random instrument in the MIDI panel.
  5. Balance challenge. Set a reference mix, then recover its balance by ear from hidden offsets. Reveal balance shows relative errors; a common volume change is ignored.

pp–ff is an intuitive playback scale. Turning down a recorded forte does not turn it into a performed piano: its timbre remains. The reference is a chosen interpretation, not a universal correct orchestration.

Space: play / pause · Esc: close a panel · Settings JSON stores filenames, seating, musician counts and reference levels. For a consistent saved library and MIDI settings, use the same browser and local server address. Local setup ↗
